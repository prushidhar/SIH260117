1. DRAWING OVERVIEW & PROCESS DESCRIPTION:
This P&ID depicts the Feed Storage, Pumping, and Heat Exchanger Unit for the Process Cooling System.
Raw feed from storage (L-101) enters through a DN50 process line (L-001) regulated by control valve FV-101.
Feed is accumulated in storage tank TK-101.
Feed liquid is pumped by centrifugal pump P-101 via bottom suction line L-002 (DN50) and discharge line L-003 (DN50) into shell-and-tube heat exchanger E-101.
Hot utility / steam enters through line L-004 (DN25) modulated by temperature control valve TV-101 based on temperature sensing from TIC-101.
Final cooled/conditioned product exits E-101 via DN50 line L-005 regulated by control valve TV-102 to product storage (L-006).
Cooling water loop is supplied from cooling tower via line L-009 to cooling water pump P-102, boosted through L-007 (DN25) into E-101, and returned to cooling tower via line L-008 through manual isolation valve XV-101.

2. VALVE INVENTORY & SPECIFICATIONS:
- FV-101: Feed Flow Control Valve
  * Tag: FV-101
  * Type: Globe / Diaphragm Control Valve (Air-to-Open, Fail-Closed)
  * Location: Line L-001 (Feed Inlet from Storage L-101)
  * Size: DN50 (2 inch)
  * Actuation: Pneumatic actuator with electro-pneumatic positioner
  * Associated Loop: Controlled by Flow Indicating Controller FIC-101, receiving signal from Flow Transmitter FT-101
  * Standard: ASME B16.34, ANSI/ISA-5.1

- TV-101: Hot Utility / Steam Temperature Control Valve
  * Tag: TV-101
  * Type: Globe Control Valve (Air-to-Open, Fail-Closed)
  * Location: Line L-004 (Hot Utility / Steam Supply to E-101)
  * Size: DN25 (1 inch)
  * Actuation: Pneumatic actuator
  * Associated Loop: Controlled by Temperature Indicating Controller TIC-101, sensing steam temperature TT-101
  * Standard: ASME B16.34, API 600

- TV-102: Product Temperature Control Valve
  * Tag: TV-102
  * Type: Angle / Globe Control Valve (Air-to-Open, Fail-Closed)
  * Location: Line L-005 (Product Outlet from E-101 to Storage L-006)
  * Size: DN50 (2 inch)
  * Actuation: Pneumatic actuator
  * Associated Loop: Controlled by Temperature Indicating Controller TIC-102, responding to product outlet temperature TT-102
  * Standard: ASME B16.34, ANSI/ISA-5.1

- XV-101: Cooling Water Return Manual Isolation Valve
  * Tag: XV-101
  * Type: Manual Ball / Gate Isolation Valve
  * Location: Line L-008 (Cooling Water Return to Cooling Tower)
  * Size: DN25 (1 inch)
  * Actuation: Handwheel / Manual Lever
  * Standard: API 600 / ASME B16.34

3. PROCESS EQUIPMENT INVENTORY:
- TK-101: Feed Storage Tank (Atmospheric accumulation vessel)
- P-101: Feed Pump (Centrifugal process pump, DN50 suction L-002, DN50 discharge L-003)
- E-101: Heat Exchanger (Shell-and-tube type, thermal energy transfer between L-003/L-005 and utility L-004/L-007)
- P-102: Cooling Water Pump (Centrifugal utility pump, suction L-009, discharge L-007 DN25)

4. INSTRUMENTATION & CONTROL LOOPS (ISA-5.1):
- Flow Control Loop 101: FT-101 (Flow Transmitter) -> FIC-101 (Flow Controller) -> FV-101 (Control Valve, Line L-001)
- Level Indication Loop 101: LT-101 (Level Transmitter) -> LIC-101 (Level Controller on TK-101)
- Pressure Indication Loop 101: PT-101 (Pressure Transmitter on discharge line L-003)
- Steam Temperature Control Loop 101: TT-101 (Temp Transmitter) -> TIC-101 (Temp Controller) -> TV-101 (Valve, Line L-004)
- Product Temperature Control Loop 102: TT-102 (Temp Transmitter on L-005) -> TIC-102 (Temp Controller) -> TV-102 (Valve, Line L-005)

5. PIPING LINES & DESIGNATIONS:
- L-001: DN50 Process Feed Inlet from Storage (L-101) to TK-101
- L-002: DN50 Process Suction line from TK-101 to P-101
- L-003: DN50 Process Discharge line from P-101 to E-101
- L-004: DN25 Steam / Hot Utility supply line to E-101
- L-005: DN50 Product outlet line from E-101 to storage
- L-006: Final product line to storage
- L-007: DN25 Cooling water supply line from P-102 to E-101
- L-008: Cooling water return line to cooling tower via XV-101
- L-009: Cooling water inlet line from cooling tower to P-102
- L-010: Hot utility (steam) supply inlet
