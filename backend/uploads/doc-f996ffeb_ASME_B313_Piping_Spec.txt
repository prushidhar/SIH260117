
ASME B31.3 PROCESS PIPING SPECIFICATION
Section 304.1.2: Straight Pipe Under Internal Pressure
For t < D/6, the internal design gage pressure shall not exceed that calculated
by the straight pipe formula:
tm = (P * D) / (2 * (S * E * W + P * Y)) + c
Where:
- P = internal design gage pressure (psig)
- D = outside diameter of pipe (inches)
- S = allowable stress value for material from Table A-1 (psi)
- E = quality factor from Table A-1A or A-1B
- W = weld joint strength reduction factor
- Y = coefficient from Table 304.1.1
- c = sum of mechanical allowances plus corrosion allowance (inches)
